Read `README.md`, `DEMO.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, the rubric in `CLAUDE.md`, `challenges/<locked>/BRIEF.md`, and the
"Pitching" section of `docs/participant-guidebook.md`.

Write `pitch/PITCH.md` containing:
1. **One-liner** (12 words or fewer) and **punchline** for the submission form.
2. **Deck outline:** 6 slides or fewer. Problem → who hurts → our solution (demo) → how it works → why it's
   credible (numbers) → what's next. Note which rubric line each slide hits.
3. **2:00 video script** (hard limit): a timed table with columns time, voice-over and screen. Aim for 1:50.
4. **3:00 stage pitch:** the live-demo version, with what to do if the demo fails.
5. **Likely judge questions** (5), with 1-line answers.

Use only claims that the repo or demo can back up. Mark anything unverified with ⚠️.
