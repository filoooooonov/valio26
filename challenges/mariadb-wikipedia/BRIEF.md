# Wikipedia Through Time (MariaDB, track 109)

**Problem:** Wikipedia runs on MariaDB, but its search is keyword-based and nobody explores its edit history. Build a
Wikipedia tool that doesn't exist yet (or is badly underused), with **MariaDB at the core**.

**Tracks:**
- **RAG** (recommended): MariaDB native vector search lets users ask questions of Wikipedia and get grounded, source-cited answers.
- **SVT** (advanced): MariaDB system-versioned tables show how knowledge changes over time (point-in-time views, edit wars, rewrites).
- **Both** (very advanced): e.g. "what did it say in 2015, and what has changed since?"

**Data:** small, medium and large **Finnish** Wikipedia datasets on the history of Finland. Use a multilingual embedding model;
you don't need to speak Finnish. Two kinds of user: readers, and active Wikipedians.

**Judging (verbatim from the brief):** effective/creative use of MariaDB RAG and/or system-versioned tables, technical execution
and scalability, real-world usefulness, design and demo quality. **Extra recognition for SVT or both tracks.**

**Sponsor wants:** tools that make knowledge easier to digest, verify and trust (source-grounded Q&A, fact-checking,
gap detection, cross-language comparison), plus time-travel tools (diffs, edit-war or bias detection, edit analytics).
